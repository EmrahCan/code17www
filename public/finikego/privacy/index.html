FinikeGo Privacy Policy

Application: FinikeGo (iOS / iPadOS) • Build: 9+ • Publisher: Code17 Technology LLC • Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Welcome to FinikeGo. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the FinikeGo mobile software application ("Application"), developed and published by Code17 Technology LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data in full compliance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines (specifically Guideline 5.1.1 on Data Collection and Storage), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Turkish KVKK Law No. 6698.

Privacy Summary: FinikeGo does NOT require registration, does NOT collect personal identifiers (such as name, phone, or email), does NOT track you across apps/websites, and does NOT sell or share any user data with advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

A. Precise Location Data (GPS):

  • Purpose: Used exclusively while actively viewing the interactive map tab or navigating the Lycian Way (Likya Yolu) trail routes. Your GPS location enables real-time on-screen positioning and calculates approximate travel distances to nearby coves, bays, ancient ruins, and municipal points of interest.
  • Storage & Transmission: Location coordinates are processed strictly on-device in volatile runtime memory. Location coordinates are never stored on remote servers, never logged, and never transmitted across the network.
  • Background Tracking: FinikeGo does not track or collect your location when the app is closed or running in the background.

B. Camera Access (QR Code Scanning):

  • Purpose: Used solely to scan official informational QR markers positioned at archaeological excavations (e.g., Arykanda, Limyra) and cultural landmarks to trigger the AI-powered audio guide narration.
  • Storage & Transmission: Camera access is only active while the scanner interface is on screen. No photographs, video recordings, or facial images are captured, saved, or uploaded to any server or cloud repository.

C. On-Device Local Storage:

  • The app stores lightweight preferences—such as bookmarked places (Favorites), travel pack checklists, and UI language selections (EN, TR, DE)—strictly within the sandbox storage of your iOS device (NSUserDefaults / LocalStorage).
  • This data remains solely under your control and is permanently purged if the application is uninstalled from your device.

2. Third-Party Frameworks & Services

FinikeGo uses the Mapbox SDK for high-resolution vector tile rendering and topographic display. Mapbox processes technical telemetry (such as standard IP headers required for HTTP network packet routing) in accordance with Apple-approved networking standards without creating persistent personal user profiles.

3. Advertising and Analytics

FinikeGo contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no ad banners, and no cross-app behavioral tracking. We do not use IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) and do not prompt App Tracking Transparency (ATT) because tracking is nonexistent.

4. Children’s Privacy

FinikeGo is rated for all ages (4+) and is designed for families and cultural travelers. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. User Rights & Data Erasure

Because all application data (bookmarks, checklists) resides locally on your iOS device, you maintain immediate and complete control:

  • You can revoke GPS location or Camera permissions at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Deleting the FinikeGo app instantly and permanently removes all locally stored preferences from your device.
