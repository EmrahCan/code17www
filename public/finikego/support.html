Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I enable GPS location for interactive trails?
When you open the Map tab for the first time, iOS prompts: "Allow 'FinikeGo' to use your location?". Choose "Allow While Using App". If previously denied, you can enable it under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → FinikeGo.
2. Why does the app request Camera permission?
FinikeGo uses the device camera exclusively to scan physical QR codes placed on information plaques at archaeological excavation sites (Arykanda, Limyra) and scenic outlooks. Scanning instantly activates an AI audio narration. No images or videos are recorded.
3. Can I use FinikeGo offline without cellular reception?
Yes! Historical information, audio scripts, and saved bookmarks are cached locally on your device so you can access them even in low-reception valleys along the Lycian Way.
4. How do I report a missing or inaccurate trail point?
Please submit a ticket using the quick form below or email us directly at support@code17.tech with GPS coordinates or location names.
