Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 • Code17 Technologies LLC

At Code17 Technologies LLC ("Code17", "we", "us", or "our"), accessible via https://code17.tech, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, schedule consultations, or communicate with our engineering team.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us via our online channels:

  • Contact and Inquiry Forms: Full name, professional email address, company name, phone/messenger handle, and the technical scope or details of your inquiry.
  • Audit and Calendar Booking: Name, work email address, company name, primary cloud platform (e.g. AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes), and appointment preferences submitted when scheduling an infrastructure audit.
  • Direct Voice & Communication Channels: Communication records if you contact our on-call desk via our web call widget or via email at contact@code17.tech.
  • Technical Log Data: Standard server logs including IP address, browser type, referral URL, and timestamps collected for security, DDoS prevention, and service availability monitoring.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process your data exclusively for legitimate business and contractual purposes:

  • Evaluating infrastructure requests and preparing architectural proposals or statements of work (SOW).
  • Coordinating and conducting scheduled 30-minute infrastructure audits and technical discovery sessions.
  • Providing 24/7 technical customer support and operational response.
  • Maintaining the security, performance, and integrity of our web services.

3. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable legal and accounting obligations. Inquiry and lead records from prospective clients who do not enter into a commercial agreement are typically retained for up to 12 months before being securely purged.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating our website and services, under strict confidentiality and data protection obligations:

  • Hosting and Infrastructure: Microsoft Azure and Cloudflare (hosting, SSL termination, and content delivery).
  • Calendar Scheduling: Calendly (management of calendar reservations and meeting links).
  • Email Delivery: Resend (transactional routing of contact briefs and inquiry notifications).
  • Voice Telephony: Zadarma WebRTC (browser-based voice line connectivity for technical consultations).
Zero Data Sale Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. International Data Transfers & GDPR / Privacy Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another jurisdiction with applicable data protection regulations (such as GDPR), you have specific statutory rights:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation of whether we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data when no longer needed for legal or contractual grounds.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request suspension of processing under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your provided data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on our legitimate interests.

6. Security Practices

We enforce rigorous administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our operational procedures are aligned with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 security principles.
